About Frenemies

We believe your pets deserve gourmet dining — not just kibble. Born in Cebu, made with love for every cat and dog who shares a household.

Where the Story Began

It started with a cat named Luna and a dog named Bruno living under the same roof, tolerating each other just enough to share dinner time. Their owner noticed one glaring injustice: premium dining options for pets in Cebu were almost non-existent.

So Frenemies Fine Dining was born — a brand dedicated to giving both cats and dogs the gourmet meals they deserve, crafted from real, wholesome ingredients.

Every recipe is vet-approved, locally sourced when possible, and made with the same care you'd give a meal for your own table.
